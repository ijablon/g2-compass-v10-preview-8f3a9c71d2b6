Trigger Map

Two 15-month windows two years apart, so every point rests on thousands of conversations.
All four are real. Counts from G2 Digital Markets' qualified buyer conversations, minimum 50 per point, no individual buyer identified. The two solid grey lines on each map sit at the middle of that map's own points, so half fall either side and they move as the market moves. Where a dashed line appears, it marks no change.
